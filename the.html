the hero's ernie!

10 episode fantasy adventure show

“After a mysterious wizard appears in his village, a reluctant baker boy is forced to become the GREATEST HERO OF ALL TIME, and with the help of his friends save the land of Iterra.”

over eight months i worked as a showrunner and led a team of 6 amazing artists to create an AWESOME, whimsical, and heartwarming show pitch. we were under the mentorship of Nickelodeon Studios and at the end of the program, had a 57-page Pitch Bible, a fully storyboarded 14-minute animatic, one minute of coloured animation, and a full understanding of the TV animation development process.
